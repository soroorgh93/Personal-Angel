# Home nursery / childcare monitoring policy (demo household policy)

## Aggressive handling of a child
If the caregiver is observed striking, shaking or roughly handling the child, or the child is heard crying
persistently after such contact, notify both parents immediately with the evidence clip and timestamps, and
notify the on-call family contact. If the behaviour repeats or the child appears injured, call emergency
services. Do not interact with the caregiver through the speaker.

## Infant fall
If the infant falls from a bed, sofa or changing table, notify the parents immediately and ask the caregiver
through the speaker to check the child. If nobody responds within 20 seconds, call emergency services.

## Nothing to report
Ordinary play, feeding, changing and comforting must not generate alerts. When unsure, request human review
rather than escalating.
