# Facility / home safety monitoring policy (demo site policy)

## Falls of elderly residents
A fall followed by more than two seconds on the ground is an incident. Ask the person through the speaker
whether they are okay. If there is no response, call emergency services and notify the family contact. If the
person responds and says they are fine, notify the family contact only.

## Visible weapons
A visible firearm anywhere on the premises is an emergency: alert police with the live location and evidence
and notify security. A visible knife outside the kitchen is escalated to on-site security first.

## Threatening or hateful speech (voicemail, calls, in-room audio)
A recorded explicit threat of violence (any language) must be translated to English, preserved as evidence
and reported to police as a non-emergency report; the target must be advised not to engage. Hateful speech
without a threat is escalated to the owner/manager for review, not to police.

## Data handling
All analysis runs on the local edge device. Recordings never leave the device; alerts contain only the
minimal evidence needed.
