# Autonomous vehicle cabin safety policy (demo operator policy)

## Passenger medical distress
If a passenger reports feeling unwell, shows a slumped/unresponsive posture, or sounds of pain are heard,
the cabin agent must first ask the passenger in their own language whether they want to be taken to the
nearest emergency room. If the passenger says no and is responsive, continue the route and re-check every
two minutes. If the passenger says yes, asks for help, or does not answer within 20 seconds, perform a safe
pull-over, reroute to the nearest emergency room and call emergency services with the vehicle location.

## Weapons in the cabin
If a firearm or knife is visibly carried by a passenger, do not address the passenger. Continue driving
normally, alert police with the live location and the evidence frames, and unlock doors only at the
destination or when instructed by police. Never confront.

## Privacy
Video and audio never leave the vehicle. Only the alert record (timestamp, location, event type, evidence
frames) is shared with emergency services or police.
